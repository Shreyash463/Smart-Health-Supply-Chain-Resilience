BRICS Track 3 Architectural Standard: Mutual Supply Chain Resilience

Interoperability & Sovereign Cross-Border Cooperation

In this multi-district architecture, each participating municipality or nation-state (represented here by Pimpri-Chinchwad and regional partner Nation B) hosts an autonomous instance of this exact schema and API. No raw patient records, personally identifiable medical data, or proprietary procurement records cross network boundaries. Instead, decentralized nodes publish standard aggregated stockout risk indicators and surplus capacity signals via an open REST/JSON contract. This enables immediate peer-to-peer early warnings, automated inter-district redistribution recommendations, and joint emergency procurement — resolving supply chain bottlenecks before they trigger frontline healthcare stockouts while strictly preserving local data sovereignty.
