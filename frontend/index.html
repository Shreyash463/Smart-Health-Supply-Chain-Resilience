Interoperability and cooperation architecture

Decentralized cross-network resilience without sovereign data exposure

Each nation or participating district deploys an autonomous instance of this standard schema and FastAPI service. Individual patient records, confidential clinic consults, and internal procurement documents strictly remain within the host jurisdiction. Participating networks publish only high-level aggregated forecast signals (such as facilities facing stockout runways under 5 days and neighboring surplus availability) via a standardized open API contract. This peer-to-peer early warning design empowers public health officers to execute timely lateral inventory redistribution across jurisdictional borders while guaranteeing complete data sovereignty.

Privacy model: Zero raw patient data exposure
Data contract: Standardized REST/JSON forecast bus
AI orchestration: Google Gemini Flash (gemini-flash-latest)
